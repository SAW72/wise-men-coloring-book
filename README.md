## Greek & Hebrew Context Notes

- **μάγοι (magoi)** – Magi / wise men. Persian/Babylonian priestly scholars, astrologers, advisors (cf. Daniel). Not “kings” in the text; number not specified as three (tradition from the three gifts).
- **ἀστέρα (astera)** – star. “His star” (αὐτοῦ τὸν ἀστέρα).
- **προσκυνῆσαι (proskynēsai)** – to worship / bow down / prostrate oneself (used for divine homage).
- **χρυσόν (chrysón)** – gold → royalty / King.
- **λίβανον (libanon)** – frankincense (from Heb. **לְבוֹנָה lebonah** “white”) → temple incense / deity / worship.
- **σμύρναν (smyrna)** – myrrh (from Heb. **מֹר mor**) → embalming / sacrifice / suffering (used at the cross & burial).
- Micah 5:2 (Heb.) prophecy of Bethlehem as birthplace of the ruler who shepherds Israel.

## How to Print

2. Print on letter or A4 paper (cardstock preferred).
3. Color with pencils, crayons, or markers.
4. Optional: make a booklet by double-sided printing and stapling.
